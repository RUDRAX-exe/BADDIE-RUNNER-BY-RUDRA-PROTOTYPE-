# Baddie Runner visual bible

Rudra: red jacket, confident eyebrows, energetic forward lean.
Lakshya: blue hoodie, wide-eyed comic panic, lighter running pose.
Arsh: green jacket, mischievous grin, sideways dodging pose.
Baddies: purple/crimson cartoon troublemakers with exaggerated eyes and silly expressions.

Maps:
1. Neon Bazaar — glowing signs, stalls, night colors.
2. Rooftop Mayhem — city skyline, vents, water tanks.
3. Candy Canyon — giant candy rocks, bright sky.
4. Sunset Road — warm road, trees, long shadows.

Keep all art colorful, funny, non-realistic, and readable on small screens.
